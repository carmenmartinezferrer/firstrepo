## Numbers and bases

- Cotton +90%, cashmere +80%, harness boots +476%: Carmen's own search pulls, worldwide, "this year against last" (`drafts/fw27-forecast-colour-draft.md`). Exact dates and whether Google only: to confirm.
- Fringe skirt +71%, paisley scarf +64%, jacquard jacket +61%: search change on last year, FW27 forecast report as printed (`drafts/fw27-forecast-trendalytics-draft.md`). Jacquard is also listed as Carmen's own pull in the colour draft: to confirm.
- Scarf: tagged in 67 of 997 Paris SS27 looks (7%), at 12 of 16 houses (75%), Dior 24 of 63 looks. Cummerbund or sash: 24 looks at 6 houses, 11 at Valentino. Counted from `dfb-data/data/SS27/paris/*.json` on 24 Nov 2026.
- The dataset tags "scarf", not the fibre, so it cannot say how many were silk. "Silk scarf" is Carmen's pick, not the runway count.
- Plum purple +60%: Carmen's own search pull, body of `drafts/fw27-forecast-colour-draft.md`. The same draft's notes say +50% on Google and +154% on TikTok: to confirm before posting.
- Left out to stay at 6 slides: fur scarf +30%, chunky belt +93%, Celine round sunglasses +190%, the "what I wouldn't gift" slide.

Deinfluencing you with data: the only things I'd buy this Black Friday.

Every gift guide wants you to buy more, so I built mine from what I've actually measured this year, a year of search data and the 997 Paris SS27 looks I tagged by hand, to help you buy less and better.

→ Natural fibres first: searches for cotton are up 90% and cashmere 80% on last year

Which one is going on your list? 👇
