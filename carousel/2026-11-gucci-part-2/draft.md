## Slide 2, The cut (small label: "The price")

**One bag, minus 30%**

In part 1 I asked whether the problem was the clothes or the price, and it turns out Gucci has been quietly answering that question itself. The Padlock small bag in France went from EUR 3,500 to EUR 2,450, which is a 30% cut, according to Bernstein's analysis as reported by Forbes. Nobody at Gucci announced it, shoppers spotted it on the price listings.

Chart idea: two bars, EUR 3,500 and EUR 2,450, 30% marked on the second one. Chart subtitle: "Padlock small bag, France, price in euros".

## Slide 3, How far it goes (small label: "The catch")

**Is it just the one bag?**

It's not clear yet, and the reports don't agree with each other. For the Mercato tote, the same one that is $3,290 on part 1, other outlets put the cut at 20% to 25%, a figure that Bernstein first flagged back in May. One Black Friday guide says the new Demna lines weren't reduced at all. Louis Vuitton, for its part, isn't following Gucci down.

Say plainly on the slide: Gucci has confirmed none of these numbers.
