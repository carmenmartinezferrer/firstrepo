## Slide 2, The cut (small label: "The price")

**Same bag, 30% cheaper**

In part 1 I asked whether the problem was the clothes or the price, and Gucci seems to have answered with its price tags. The Beatrix large tote in brown suede cost $3,300 in the US in October 2025 and costs $2,310 now, in November 2026, which is exactly 30% less. It's reportedly not just the one bag, since carryover styles like T-shirts, sneakers and handbags are said to have come down by up to 30% in Europe, the US and China, and Gucci hasn't announced any of it.

Chart: two bars, $3,300 (October 2025) and $2,310 (November 2026), 30% marked on the second. Chart subtitle: "Gucci Beatrix large tote, brown suede, US price in dollars".

## Slide 3, Why now (small label: "The reason")

**The middle-class shopper stopped coming**

Kering's CEO Luca de Meo has admitted that price rises may have gone further than they needed to, and the numbers from part 1 show why that matters, with sales in Gucci's own stores down 9% in Q3. According to Bernstein, as reported by the Wall Street Journal, both Gucci and Louis Vuitton make more than half of their sales from middle-class shoppers who spend roughly $2,000 to $3,000 a year on occasional treats like a handbag or a piece of jewellery, and growth in that mid-price part of luxury has evaporated.
