## Data

- +180%, +130%, +160%, +30%: Carmen's own year on year readings from Google Trends. The exports behind them are not saved yet.
- Line chart: `mandarin-jacket-weekly.csv`, her 12 month weekly export (16 Nov 2025 to 15 Nov 2026), drawn as a 3 week average, no index.
- Jacquard jackets +61%: searches vs last year as printed in the Trendalytics Womenswear Forecast Fall/Winter 2027, via her forecast article.
- 8 of 11 and the runway looks: FashionUnited's SS27 edit.
- Palette: photo samples from her Conner Ives pictures, not Pantone codes.
