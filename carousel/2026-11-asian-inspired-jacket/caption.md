Asianmaxxing? The next jacket of 2027 might be at a vintage shop right now.

It started in February with Chinamaxxing, the TikTok trend of adopting Chinese habits as a lifestyle (house slippers, warm water, Tai Chi). The meme faded in weeks, but people kept the clothes.

→ Searches for "mandarin jacket" are up 180% on last year, and "opera jacket" 130%
→ Fashion has borrowed it for decades, since Saint Laurent in 1977, and what is new is how we see China
→ It is working now because it comes with maximalism: fur trim jackets up 160%, jacquard jackets up 61%
→ And it comes in soft colours: ivory, gold, blossom pink and butter yellow

My take: we are still obsessed with the Asian aesthetic, and it is here to stay in 2027. The frog jacket had its big spike in April, and since fashion week it is the Conner Ives version we want, with fur, jacquard and embroidery. The jacket is the accessory now, and the best part is that you don't need to buy a new one, because vintage shops are full of them!
