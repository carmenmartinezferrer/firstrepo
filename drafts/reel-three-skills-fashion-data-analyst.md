**Full script (read-through, about 100 words, roughly 40 seconds):**

I use AI for the automated stuff, like coding, but the three skills that make me valuable as a fashion data analyst have nothing to do with it. AI makes information and output cheap and endless, so the people who matter are the ones who can make sense of the noise and make people care. Number one is asking the right question, because a spreadsheet will happily answer a bad question perfectly. Number two is finding a proxy when the data is not there, since nobody hands you a number for what people want to wear, so I use Google Trends as a stand-in for interest and put every candidate on the same scale. And number three, the one I would put first if I could, is storytelling, very simple storytelling that hooks people, because the best analysis in the world changes nothing if nobody gets past the first ten seconds. None of this needs a new tool, which is exactly why anyone can learn it, so which one is already yours?

## Why the three sit in this order (the peak curve)

Asking the right question is the most recognisable, so it opens the climb and lets the viewer nod. The proxy is the surprise because it shows a real workaround with a named tool. Storytelling goes last because it is your strongest opinion and the one the viewer is most likely to repeat, and the close then hands them something to answer in the comments rather than summarising.
