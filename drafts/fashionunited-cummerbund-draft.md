The cummerbund, the pleated sash traditionally worn with a man's tuxedo, has crossed into womenswear and become the accessory of the party season, worn over shirts, knitwear, dresses and jeans to define the waist and give a plain outfit some personality. [SEARCH DATA TO ADD: one sentence with Carmen's Google Trends figure for "cummerbund", period and comparison stated.]

The shift started on the Spring/Summer 2027 runways, where the cummerbund appeared in 38 percent of the Paris collections, according to The Data Fashion Brief's analysis, as the clearest expression of a waist that designers had brought back into focus. What makes it relevant for the holiday period is how little it asks of the customer, since it turns a shirt and trousers or a simple dress into an evening look without replacing any of them, and many shoppers already own something that does the same job.

## Paris brings the waist back

Based on The Data Fashion Brief's analysis of Paris Fashion Week SS27, belted waists and corset-style tops appeared in 88 percent of the collections and almost half of all looks included a fitted piece, after several seasons led by soft and oversized shapes. The cummerbund is the easiest way to get that waist, because it sits over a shirt, a knit or a blazer without changing what is underneath.

Valentino made the strongest case, with almost half of the looks in Alessandro Michele's show featuring a version of it, from lace and suede cummerbunds over satin bustiers to satin sashes over pleated trousers. Balenciaga took the opposite approach under Pierpaolo Piccioli, with ultra-wide satin sashes in teal and red worn low on the hips with oversized blazers. The turning point came earlier in the season at Celine's menswear show, where satin cummerbunds in fuchsia and sky blue were worn over plain crew-neck knitwear and relaxed trousers, and that formula, one strong accessory over a quiet outfit, is the one womenswear has since adopted.

## An Indian accessory, long before the tuxedo

The cummerbund is also one of several Indian references that Western fashion weeks have drawn on recently, alongside the Kolhapuri-style sandals in Prada's Spring/Summer 2026 menswear show and the mukaish embroidery on a coat in Dior's menswear collection the same season. Its name comes from the kamarband of south Asia, from the Persian words for waist and band, a waist adornment worn in both fabric and jewelled forms. British officers stationed in colonial India adopted the fabric sash worn by Indian soldiers, and from there it entered men's eveningwear in the West and became part of the black tie uniform it is now leaving behind.

## An update that does not need a new purchase

The practical argument for the party season is that a cummerbund can be made from almost anything already in a wardrobe. It follows directly from the silk scarf belt of 2025, when printed scarves were folded and wrapped around the waist, and a scarf, a length of satin, a tie or a cummerbund from an existing formalwear set produces the same line as a new one. Marco Rambaldi made the point on the Spring/Summer 2027 runway, where printed fabric sashes were knotted over faded jeans. No resale or rental data yet isolates the cummerbund, so the case for reuse rests on how the piece is made and styled rather than on a measured figure.

## What this means for brands and retailers

For brands, the cummerbund is a low-risk way into the party season, an entry-price accessory in satin and silk that sells an evening look to a customer who is not buying a new dress. For retailers, it points towards styling it with existing stock, shirts, knitwear, tailored trousers and slip dresses, and towards treating scarves and sashes as part of the same waist story.

Not every version is likely to last equally. The Data Fashion Brief's reading is that the theatrical interpretations, such as lace worn over a bustier, may have a short moment, while the plain satin sash over tailoring behaves like a belt, a category that does not leave the wardrobe. The first test is holiday trading over the coming weeks, and the second comes when the Autumn/Winter 2027 collections are shown in February and March, where another season of defined waists would move the cummerbund from a seasonal accessory to an established one.
