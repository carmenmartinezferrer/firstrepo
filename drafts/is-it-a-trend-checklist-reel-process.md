**Full voiceover (read-through):**
I'm a fashion data analyst and this is how I tell a trend from a moment. One, I count two numbers, how many houses wear it and how much of each collection it is, because one house going all in isn't a trend. Two, I put it next to its closest rival on the same scale, because something can look huge on its own and barely register next to another. Three, I wait for the spike to pass, because what's left once the show buzz is gone is the real signal. Four, I look for a source that doesn't depend on the first one, like retail data or search, because two methods agreeing is stronger than one. Pass most of them and it's a trend, pass only one and it's a moment, and if the data can't say what the thing is, I say so. Comment CHECKLIST and I'll send you the full list.
