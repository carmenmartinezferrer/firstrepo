**Full voiceover (read-through):**
I'm a fashion data analyst and this is how I tell a trend from a moment. One, I count two numbers, how many houses wear it and how much of each collection it is, because tweed is 5% of Paris looks but 42% of Chanel's, so that's a signature, not a trend. Two, I put it next to its rival on the same scale, because the slip dress beat the midi skirt in search in 50 of the last 53 weeks, even though the midi was at every single house. Three, I wait for the spike to pass, because skinny jean searches jumped in the spring and were back at last year's level by October. Four, I look for a source that doesn't depend on the first one, like retail data, because three methods agreeing is stronger than one.
