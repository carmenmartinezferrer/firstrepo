**Full script (read-through, about 125 words):**
I'm a fashion data analyst and this is how I tell a trend from a moment. It takes five steps, and the last one matters most. One, I scrape the internet and social media to see what people are talking about. Two, I wait for the spike to pass, because what's left after the buzz is the real signal. Three, I look for an independent source, like retail data. Four, I count how many houses wear it and how much of each collection it is. And five, the one that matters most, I put it next to its closest rival on the same scale, because something can look huge alone and barely register next to another. If you want to know more, let me know.

**Cut (no goal):** the examples (every specific trend), the "pass most and it's a trend, pass one and it's a moment" rule, and the "if the data can't say what it is, I say so" line.
