# DFB Black Friday Reels: De-influencing

**Source:** Carmen's own picks from this chat (no article), plus the deal research from the same session. Every number is either Carmen's own word or flagged below.

**Core argument:** Carmen's rule for Black Friday is to buy only what outlasts a spike, which gives four buys (headphones, DataCamp, a methods book, a natural-fibre coat or sweater) and one skip (short-lived prints like zebra and dalmatian). The strongest receipt is the DataCamp 60% off, which Carmen confirmed herself; the "skip" half is her professional opinion until a head-to-head search chart backs it.

**Full script (read-through):**
As a fashion data analyst, here are four things worth your money this Black Friday, and one I'm skipping. My rule is that if something spikes and disappears I don't buy it, and if it lasts I'll happily spend more. First, good headphones, because I use mine for everything, even chatting with AI. Second, DataCamp, which is 60% off, and it built all of my technical skills. Third, a book on how to run a data project, because tools go out of date and good thinking doesn't. Fourth, a coat or a sweater in natural fibres like wool or cashmere, because that's where spending more actually pays off. And I'm skipping anything that's a trend for a moment, like zebra or dalmatian print, because I'd rather keep wearing my leopard than chase the print of the month. If it can't outlast the trend cycle, it doesn't make my cart, so what are you skipping this year?

**Sentence breakdown (every line has a job):**
- "As a fashion data analyst" _[authority tag]_ + "four things worth your money" _[promise]_ + "and one I'm skipping" _[open loop]_ → **curiosity**
- "if something spikes and disappears I don't buy it" _[rule, problem]_ + "if it lasts I'll happily spend more" _[rule, payoff]_ → **recognition** (and the frame every item is judged by)
- "good headphones" _[item]_ + "I use mine for everything, even chatting with AI" _[personal use]_ → **recognition**
- "DataCamp, which is 60% off" _[item + data]_ + "it built all of my technical skills" _[personal result]_ → **urgency and trust**
- "a book on how to run a data project" _[item]_ + "tools go out of date and good thinking doesn't" _[contrast, the insight]_ → **surprise** (it also explains why the book sits right after a course)
- "natural fibres like wool or cashmere" _[characteristic]_ + "that's where spending more actually pays off" _[result]_ → **permission to spend**
- "anything that's a trend for a moment" _[claim]_ + "zebra or dalmatian print" _[named examples]_ + "I'd rather keep wearing my leopard" _[personal contrast]_ → **the quotable peak**
- "If it can't outlast the trend cycle, it doesn't make my cart" _[reframe]_ + "what are you skipping this year?" _[values question]_ → **comments**
