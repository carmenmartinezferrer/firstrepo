## Post A: the 12 colours (carousel A1 to A7)

Stop calling everything a trend: the Fall/Winter 2027 colour edition

Ok ok ok guys, a forecast just handed me the 12 colours we'll apparently see on next autumn's runways, split into three moods: Heritage Craft (russet red, plum purple, forest green, chestnut brown), Sensory Riot (chartreuse, fuchsia, lilac, cobalt blue) and Veiled Performance (marigold, moss green, dove grey, terracotta).

The bit I care about is why these twelve, so I lined them up by the number each one comes with. Only five have an actual search volume, and marigold wins it at 126k, then lilac 103k and fuchsia 84k, with the two heritage colours last, forest green 57k and dark red 35k.

Five more only have search growth: dove grey +43%, plum purple +39%, moss green +23%, cobalt blue +14% and terracotta +7%. A colour whose searches grew 7% in a year is not a forecast, it's a colour that exists.

And two only have TikTok views, chestnut brown at 196M and chartreuse at 149M.

Twelve colours, three yardsticks, none measured twice, which is exactly why I don't call all twelve a trend. On my own Paris SS27 tagging, red was 2% of the palette and purple 1%, so the heritage palette isn't coming from the runway yet, it's coming from the fabric fairs.

I'm still buying something marigold, for the record. (your take: edit)

## Post B: the trends (carousel B1 to B7)

Stop calling everything a trend: the Fall/Winter 2027 edition

A forecast gave me 36 trends for next autumn, each with one number next to it, and every number was a different kind. So I did what I always do and put them on the same scale.

On search growth, fringe skirts lead at +71%, then paisley scarves +64% and jacquard jackets +61%, with cobalt blue (+14%) and terracotta (+7%) at the bottom. On market adoption, painterly florals are up 178% and slim suits 133%, and nothing else is close.

Then there are the nine items nobody can rank: funnel neck jackets +888% among influencers (from what base? it doesn't say), rain boots +87%, chestnut brown 196M TikTok views, and tweed and silk "most saturated in luxury" with no number at all.

My own Paris SS27 tagging backs two of them properly. Silk was in 28% of all looks across 15 of 16 houses, and embellishment (fringe included) in 31% of looks at every single house. Tweed, sorry, was 5% of looks and almost all Chanel.

36 trends, 6 yardsticks, 0 measured twice. My bet for the shops is the fringe skirt, because search, retail and the runway all say the same thing.

Which one are you already eyeing up? I need to know I'm not the only one looking at riding boots. (your take: edit)
