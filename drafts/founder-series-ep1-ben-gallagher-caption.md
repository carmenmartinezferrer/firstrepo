# DFB Founder Series, episode 1: Ben Gallagher (caption and interview questions)

Saved 26 Nov 2026. The Reel itself was scripted and filmed by Carmen, this file holds the agreed caption, the checks, and the questions sent to Fashionphile's PR.

## Caption (agreed version)

I interviewed a founder I've been following for years, and I'm screaming, crying, excited!!!

Ben Gallagher started Luxe Collective with his brother at 18, from their parents' back bedroom. Seven years, 3 million followers and £30m in sales later, he had to shut it down. Here he tells me why, and what it taught him.

Luxe Collective was bought by Fashionphile, where Ben works now, and it was so fun to film this surrounded by all the secondhand designer bag treasures!

I'm a data analyst working in fashion who is also obsessed with entrepreneurship, and above all curious, because curiosity is one of the biggest skills a data analyst can have, and I want to know it alllll!!!

If you liked it, comment PART 2, or tell me what you'd like to know from now on!

## Questions sent to the PR

1. For anyone who doesn't know you: you built Luxe Collective, and now you're at Fashionphile. What's the short version of how you got here, and what do you do now?
2. Are people coming to resale first now, before they go to the boutique?
3. Are people after a better price, or the piece nobody else has, and was it different a few years ago?
4. Are people asking for vintage and older pieces more than they used to, and what do they say about how they were made?
5. Your report had Blazy's first Chanel bags going for more than retail. Why do you think people are loving his Chanel so much?
6. Luxury brands keep getting criticised for AI-made campaigns, and at the same time I'm seeing people go back to nostalgic pieces, which is exactly what secondhand is. Do you think people are tired of things looking too perfect?

Quickfire: Dior by Galliano or Dior by Jonathan Anderson? Chanel by Karl Lagerfeld or Chanel by Matthieu Blazy? Gucci by Tom Ford or Gucci by Demna? Birkin or Kelly? Brand new or 20 years old? One bag to buy today and keep for five years?
