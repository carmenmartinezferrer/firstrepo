# Paris SS27 Reels: trend report + colour index

**What the two articles argue.** The trend report says Paris's big "trend", the pencil skirt, is in every house but carried by four of them (Chanel 49% of its looks, Dries Van Noten 40%, Balmain 33%, plus Valentino, together nearly two thirds of all pencil skirt looks), that wool suiting is the season's material and the column its shape, and that lingerie-style tops are a hidden story once you group them (12% of looks). The colour index says Paris was 56% neutral, but colour lives in a few houses: pink is the most widespread colour (8% of looks, 13 houses, ballet pink as the shade), Lacoste wrote its palette into its show title, and Miu Miu's second biggest colour, brown, is the one the reviews skipped.

All numbers are from the two drafts (paris_4 data). Whole numbers only, no dashes in on-screen text.
