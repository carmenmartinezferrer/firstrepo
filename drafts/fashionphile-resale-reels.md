# DFB Reels: Resale decides which designers are desirable (with Ben Gallagher, Fashionphile)

Draft 1, 17 Nov 2026. Source: shift 03 of "This Week in Fashion Data: 5 Shifts #15/11" (published 16 Nov), plus shift 01 (Burberry) and the Hermès and luxury slowdown posts for the context numbers. Ben's answers are not filmed yet, so every Ben line is a slot, never a quote.

**The argument in three sentences.** Luxury is priced past what the customer will pay and most houses are shrinking, with Gucci down 6% last quarter and even the Birkin 30 now reselling for what it costs in store. Against that, Fashionphile's October report found it was paying sellers 15% over the boutique price for Matthieu Blazy's first Chanel bag, the Urban Essential, and 13% over for the Souplissimo, within months of his debut. So the secondhand market put a price on the designer before the house reported a number, and the same thing shows up in the archive, where eBay has Galliano-era Dior selling for 81% more than a year ago.

## Questions for Ben (each one is built to give you a clip for a slot)

Ask them standing next to the bags, and ask him to put the question in his answer so the clip stands alone.

1. **For Ben clip 1 (why resale, why now):** "When someone walks in here, are they after a better price, or the piece nobody else has?"
2. **For Ben clip 2 (the designer effect):** "How quickly does a new creative director show up in your prices, and what happened here when Blazy got to Chanel?"
3. **To settle the number, on or off camera:** "Your report says 15% over retail for the Urban Essential. Is that what you paid the seller or what it sold for, and do you have it for the UK?"
4. **Spare, for a second cut or the Substack:** "Which bag's resale price is telling you a designer isn't working?" He may not name one, and "I can't say" is still a usable clip.
5. **Spare:** "If you had to buy one designer's era today and hold it for five years, whose?"
6. **Spare, the pricing fatigue:** "Have boutique price rises sent you customers, and can you see it in the numbers?"
