## Slide 8, the question (white page, pink kicker "The catch")

**A trend this decorative should come with a name for where it's from**

Orientalism is the word for Western culture presenting "the East" as one decorative, faraway place, and the critics using it about fashion are usually pointing at the blurring. One 2026 critique of Valentino's Resort 2026 collection says its references merge into one vague, distant "Orient" instead of naming specific cultures [S7], and another says Prada only called a South Asian inspired design "inspired" after public backlash [S7].

Neither is a Chinese reference, so I'm not claiming the opera coat is the same case. The test I'd use is whether the garment or craft is actually named, a Qing tapestry or a Peking opera robe, rather than "Oriental" or "exotic".
