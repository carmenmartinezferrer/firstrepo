# A Coruña Reels, draft v1 (text only, for Carmen to correct)

**What I understood:** you are walking the streets of A Coruña this weekend and want a Reel about the city itself, a small, elegant, señorial place on the Atlantic that turned into a rare international fashion hub because of Inditex, with real data in it, and with Zara mentioned honestly but not promoted. There is no article behind this one, so every number below comes from a public source I looked up, and anything I could not pin down is marked NEEDS DATA or [CHECK].

**Two things in your brief that the sources do not support as stated, so I wrote around them:**
- **"Amancio is from there".** He was born in Busdongo de Arbas, in León, and grew up in A Coruña after his family moved there when he was a child (sources disagree on the exact age). The scripts say "grew up here", never "born here".
- **"The whole city works for Inditex".** The headquarters is in Arteixo, about 13 km outside the city, and press estimates put it at roughly 5,000 people, in a city of about 249,000. That is around 2 in every 100, so I turned the claim into the myth the Reel checks (Reel 2), which is honestly a better hook than repeating it.

I wrote three Master formula Reels with three different angles. I did not write a Comparison Reveal, because nothing here is a real two-sided comparison with a measurable winner.

**Angle:** everyone names Paris, Milan, London and New York, and nobody names the small Atlantic city where one of the biggest fashion groups in the world is actually run.

**Full script (read-through, 128 words, about 50 seconds at a relaxed pace):**

Paris, Milan, London, New York, and then a city of about 249,000 people in the rainy corner of Spain that nobody puts on the list. I'm in A Coruña this weekend, and I'd argue it belongs on that list. The first Zara opened here in 1975, on a street where people still do their food shopping, and the group behind it is still run from an industrial estate 13 kilometres from here. That group now employs around 163,000 people of 174 nationalities across 57 markets, which is why you hear four languages here before you finish your coffee. You can think what you like about fast fashion, and I have thoughts, but fashion's capitals are wherever the decisions get made, and a lot of them get made here.

**Sentence breakdown (every line has a job):**
- "Paris, Milan, London, New York" _[context everyone knows]_ + "a city of about 249,000 people... nobody puts on the list" _[contrast + data]_ → **curiosity**
- "I'd argue it belongs on that list" _[claim]_ → **tension**, the viewer wants the proof
- "The first Zara opened here in 1975" _[named evidence]_ + "where people still do their food shopping" _[characteristic, it is an ordinary street]_ → **recognition**
- "still run from an industrial estate 13 kilometres from here" _[data + contrast with the glamour of the other four]_ → **surprise**
- "163,000 people of 174 nationalities across 57 markets" _[hard stat]_ → **scale**, the top of the climb
- "four languages before you finish your coffee" _[result you can hear]_ → **proof at street level**
- "You can think what you like about fast fashion, and I have thoughts" _[distance from the brand]_ + "capitals are wherever the decisions get made" _[reframe]_ → **quotable close**

**Cut (no goal):** the sales figure (it reads as promotion here and Reel 2 uses it better), the Amancio biography, any greeting.

**Quotable close:** "Fashion's capitals are wherever the decisions get made."
**Comment prompt:** Which city would you add to the list?

**Caption:**
Four fashion capitals everyone can name, and a fifth one nobody does.
I'm in A Coruña this weekend, where a group of 163,000 people and 174 nationalities is run from 13 km outside a city of about 249,000.

**Full script (read-through, 124 words, about 50 seconds at a relaxed pace):**

Everyone told me the whole city works for Zara, so obviously I went and checked. It turns out the real story is better than the myth. The headquarters sits 13 kilometres outside town, and press estimates put it at around 5,000 people, in a city of about 249,000, which is roughly 2 in every 100. Those few thousand come from all over the world though, to the point where there's a local nickname for them, Zarasmus, and last year the group they work for sold almost 40 billion euros of clothes. So no, not everyone here works in fashion, it's that a small, very elegant city on the Atlantic quietly became one of the most international offices in the industry. Would you move here?

**Sentence breakdown (every line has a job):**
- "Everyone told me the whole city works for Zara" _[the myth]_ + "so obviously I went and checked" _[analyst reflex, a bit of humour]_ → **curiosity**
- "the real story is better than the myth" _[promise]_ → **reason to stay**
- "13 kilometres outside town" _[data]_ + "around 5,000 people" _[data, attributed]_ → **context**
- "in a city of about 249,000, which is roughly 2 in every 100" _[data + result]_ → **surprise**, the myth falls
- "from all over the world" _[characteristic]_ + "Zarasmus" _[named detail]_ → **delight**, the line people repeat in the comments
- "sold almost 40 billion euros of clothes" _[hard stat]_ → **scale**, top of the climb
- "not everyone here works in fashion" _[resolution]_ + "one of the most international offices in the industry" _[reframe]_ + "Would you move here?" _[CTA]_ → **comments**

**Full script (read-through, 112 words, about 45 seconds at a relaxed pace):**

This is the street where the first Zara in the world opened, and the shop is being turned into a supermarket. I think that tells you more about this company than any store count. Amancio Ortega, who by the way was born in León and grew up here, opened it on the 9th of May 1975, and it closed this January after half a century. The group has 5,460 stores around the world, and it didn't keep the one where everything started. Whatever you think of fast fashion, that's the whole model in one shopfront, because nothing gets to stay just because it worked before. Is that ruthless, or is it discipline?

**Sentence breakdown (every line has a job):**
- "the street where the first Zara in the world opened" _[context]_ + "being turned into a supermarket" _[contrast]_ → **curiosity**
- "tells you more about this company than any store count" _[claim]_ → **tension**
- "born in León and grew up here" _[small myth busted]_ + "9th of May 1975" _[named date]_ → **credibility**
- "closed this January after half a century" _[data + contrast]_ → **surprise**
- "5,460 stores" _[hard stat]_ + "didn't keep the one where everything started" _[contrast]_ → **the peak**
- "the whole model in one shopfront" _[reframe]_ + "ruthless, or is it discipline?" _[open question]_ → **comments**

**Cut (no goal):** the reason for the closure (I found the date and what replaces it, not an official reason, so the script does not guess one), the nationality and sales figures.
