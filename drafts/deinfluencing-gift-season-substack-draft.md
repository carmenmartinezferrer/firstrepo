# Deinfluencing You With Data: The Only Things I'd Buy This Gift Season

Subtitle: Wool, a midi skirt, a plum anything and one silk scarf, which is honestly the whole list.

Status: text draft v1, 26 Nov 2026, waiting for Carmen's corrections and product links. No visuals yet.

---

Every gift guide in your inbox this week wants you to buy more, and this one wants you to buy less (I know, terrible business model). I've spent the year pulling search data and tagging 997 Paris runway looks by hand, so before buying anything this season I checked what the numbers actually back, and the list turned out to be short.

### Buy the fabric, not the logo

Searches for wool are up 100% on last year, cotton 90% and cashmere 80%, which I read as people checking the label inside before the one outside. If you buy one knit, look at the composition first, because a plain wool jumper will outlast any logo. [LINK: Carmen's pick]

### Buy it in the new neutrals

Plum is up 60% in searches this year, dark red and forest green 50% each and cobalt blue 30%, and these are the colours I'd treat as neutrals now, the ones that go with everything you already own. [LINK: Carmen's pick]

### The one piece worth investing in

Searches for midi skirts are up 250% on last year, which is the biggest number on this list and also the least exciting item on it, a basic you'll wear for years. Fur trim jackets are up 160% and paisley scarves 64%, if you want something with more texture. [LINK: Carmen's pick]

### The small things

Accessories are moving faster than the clothes, with searches for Celine's round sunglasses up 190%, chunky belts up 93% and fur scarves up 30%, so this is where a small budget goes furthest. [LINK: Carmen's pick]

### And if you only buy one thing

Make it a silk scarf. Three in four of the Paris houses I tagged showed a scarf at least once, and it works at the neck, in the hair, on a bag or tied at the waist as a cummerbund, which is my favourite trend right now. [LINK: Carmen's pick]

So before you buy anything this month, ask whether it's a natural fibre, whether it's a colour you'll still wear in two years, and whether you'd want it without the discount, and if the answer is no, you can leave it in the basket.
