# Runway Colour Combos: Green SS27, Reels

## Reel 1: Green + a dark neutral

Everyone says green is hard to wear, but the runway shows it's all about what you pair it with. I measured how much of each look is actually green, and at Conner Ives it's 30% lime and 70% black, Gucci goes 36% sage and 64% black, and Moschino does 42% lime with 58% oxblood. Then Loewe splits it almost in half, 53% mint and 47% chocolate suede! So the trick is one green piece with a dark neutral doing the heavy lifting. Would you wear it?

## Reel 2: Green + more colour

Green showed up at 8% of Paris Fashion Week SS27 and 11% of New York, and some designers paired it with even more colour. Tory Burch went 50% teal green and 50% turquoise, a perfect split of two shades most of us would never put together. Dries Van Noten did 75% teal with 25% gold, so the gold is just a flash at the top. And then Moschino, which I did not see coming, 46% jade and 54% chocolate plum. Copy the ratio, not just the colours, and green gets so much easier. Which one would you wear?

## How the percentages were measured

Share of the photo's garment area matching each named colour (within a colour-distance threshold), counting only the two named colours, so each pair adds to 100%. Skin, hair, bags and other garments are excluded. Shiny fabric (Dries gold satin, Moschino plum) loses some highlight pixels, so those shares lean slightly low. Approximate, rounded to whole numbers.
