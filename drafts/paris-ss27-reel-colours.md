# Reel: The colours of the season, and why

I'm a fashion data analyst, and it's my job to tell you the colours of the season, and why!

Starting with purple, which showed up in 9% of the New York Fashion Week looks I analysed, and it's up 23% in popularity year on year.

Then pink!!! It showed up at 13 of the 16 Paris shows, in 8% of all looks, and ballet pink was the shade, with Isabel Marant and Valentino going the furthest.

Then green, in every shade you can think of, khaki, olive, mint and bottle green, at 15 of the 16 Paris houses, and Lacoste and Loewe built a quarter of their palettes around it.

Then the evolution of butter yellow, which grew up into lemon, ochre and chartreuse, at 14 of the 16 Paris houses.

Then blue, from powder blue to royal blue, in 9% of Paris looks.

And finally red! It was everywhere in New York, at 9% of the season, but in Paris it only made 2% of the palette, so it's a colour for the few houses that went all in, like Chanel and Lanvin.
