The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere, the colour report

The surprise for me is blue and navy at 13%, the second colour of the night, when it was only 3% at the Academy Museum Gala. I've been saying blue is one key colour for 2027, moving from bright blues into dustier, darker ones, so I'm taking this one as a win!

Nude, cream and white add up to 21%, all tulle, champagne satin and feathers, and then come green and gold at 17% and brown and burgundy at 16%, which I did not expect at a premiere this theatrical.

And red was under 1%, when at the Academy Museum Gala it was 15% and the real star of the night, so I'm very curious where it went.
