I analysed the colours at The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere, so here's what people actually wore

**Slide 2, donut**
Title: The Sunrise on the Reaping premiere colour palette
Footnote: % = share of all outfit colour across the 45 looks I measured

**Slide 3**
Title: Black, 23%
Black at 23%, still the biggest colour on the carpet, although it was 28% when I measured the Academy Museum Gala, so even at a Capitol premiere the classic base loses a little ground.

**Slide 4**
Title: Blue and navy, 13%
Blue and navy at 13%, the second colour of the night, which runs from the navy suits to a royal blue velvet gown and a pair of feather wings, and it was only 3% at the gala.

**Slide 5**
Title: Nude, cream and white, 21%
Nude and beige at 11% and white and cream at 10%, which means tulle, champagne satin and feathers took up a fifth of the carpet, and it's the soft side of the Capitol.

**Slide 6**
Title: Brown and burgundy, 16%
Brown at 9% and burgundy at 7%, and I did not expect the autumn darks at a premiere this theatrical, but chocolate suits, burgundy coats and velvet were everywhere.

**Slide 7**
Title: Green and gold, 17%
Green at 9% and gold at 8%, mostly liquid satin and sequins, and honestly these were the looks that felt the most Capitol to me.

**Slide 8**
Title: Everything else, 10%
Pink 5%, grey and silver 4%, purple 1%, and red under 1%, when it was 15% and the real star at the Academy Museum Gala. Which one would you wear?
