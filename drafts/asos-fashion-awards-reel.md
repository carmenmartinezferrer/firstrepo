**Full script (read-through):**
If you think party dressing has been a bit boring lately, I have good news for the upcoming Fashion Awards on Monday, because quiet luxury isn't invited, and neither am I! As a data analyst working in fashion, ASOS asked me for my biggest prediction of the night, and my answer is maximalism, and the clearest way we'll see it is through decoration. And how do I know? Because it will be all about fringe, sequins and beading, and I think it's one of the biggest shifts we're going to see: fashion that looks handmade, like someone put real effort into it, which is also why it feels so nostalgic, like it's from another era. In my Paris fashion week analysis for 2027, decoration was on 31% of the looks. Fringe and beading, like this skirt, sequins, like this top, and both looks are from ASOS. And Monday is where it should show, but do you even know what the Fashion Awards are? They're the British Fashion Council's big night, running since 1989, so it's a great place to see which trends people actually dare to wear. So if you were worried about looking overdressed on Monday, the data says relax, so which look is yours, one or two?

(About 210 words, roughly 60 seconds. For about 50 seconds, cut "like it's from another era" and shorten the history to "the British Fashion Council's big night, running since 1989".)
