# 2026 Summer Trend Autopsy
## dissecting the origins of beaded bags, bug-eyed sunnies, and jelly...everything

Summer is whimsy.

It's a time where rigid dressing is completely thrown out the window in favor of a lighter, airier kind of presentation. It bewitches you, body and soul.

I can't tell you the number of times I've been in a "minimalist" mindset, swearing to myself that the prospect of summer won't persuade me to buy that new, hot tote bag or that kitschy little pair of crab earrings. But alas, I am influenced all over again, without fail. As much as I try to escape my island roots/beach town upbringing, it rears its ugly head by the time the Atlanta heat wave sets in.

This year's summer fashion trends almost feel like a deliberate rejection of the Q1 CBK effect that took the world by storm due to the success of FX's Love Story. Or maybe Carolyn Bessette's style, more so, lends itself to cooler weather, camel coats, loafers, dark wash denim, solid neutrals, etc.

Either way, we've entered a new season of life, of style, and of exploration.

So, let's talk about the trends shaping summer fashion, right here, right now. But I want to do more than just give you a list of trends, because that would be boring. I'm all for a round-up, but we need context. We need substance. We need criticism. Fashion without friction is meaningless, in my humble opinion.

I'm here to dissect. A trend autopsy, if you will, except they are all alive and well.

You'll also notice that many of these trends are actually spin-offs of last summer's familiar trends, but with a slight tweak or evolution.

As someone who hates the whiplash of a microtrend, it's a breath of fresh air to see certain styles stick around for longer, even if they are being slightly upgraded in different ways. Maybe society is healing after all? Doubtful, but I'll take my wins.

Let's dig in.

## Trend #1: Beaded Woven Bags

### Woven/netted beaded bags

Simple mesh totes were all the rage last year, but now it's time to up the ante. The summer bag needs to be a little more personalized, and a little more durable than just a thin, mesh number you take to the grocery store and plop two oranges in (for the aesthetic). Whether crochet, raffia, fishnet, rope, or woven canvas, pretty much every brand has its own formula for this kind of bag, and beads are fitting into many of the equations.

### The Scene of the Crime: Zara Beaded Bucket Bag

This trend hit me over the head when I kept seeing the Zara Beaded Bucket Bag online, everywhere. Now sold out (of course), this bag pretty much sums up Summer 2026 in one, tightly woven little package. The hard handle, the multi-colored beads, the cream weave, simple but effective. It has a certain element of craftiness that summer trends tend to have. The more handmade-looking, the better. The irony is that now, every other affordable brand is ripping off Zara.

### The Paper Trail: Fendi Spring 2026

Silvia Venturini Fendi's last show before stepping down as creative director was something truly magical. Other brands like Stella McCartney, Tory Burch, Giorgia Armani, and Bodi introduced unique woven bags/beaded bags in their Spring 2026 collections, but nothing as "in full force" as Fendi.

### The Verdict: Diverse Enough Not to Be Cringe

This trend, done right, is fabulous. It's not super forced. It feels like the natural progression of fashion, rather than a weird thing the algorithm is forcing down our throats. There are also so many different iterations and diverse designs to where you can really forge your own path here.
