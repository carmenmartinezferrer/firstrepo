## Caption

Everyone asked me what replaces the barrel jean, so I tagged every look from Paris and New York SS27 and put it next to a year of Google searches. The answer isn't a new shape. 77% of Paris denim was cut relaxed, the straight leg beat the wide leg 3 to 1, and "straight jeans" is already searched 3x more than "barrel". The styling formula is jeans + blazer (Tom Ford, Margiela, Stella). The barrel isn't over, it's just not the story anymore. Full breakdown with all the numbers on my Substack, link in bio.
