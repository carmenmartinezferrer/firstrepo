# Reels: The jean you'll keep seeing in 2027 is probably already in your wardrobe

**The argument.** After the barrel, the SS27 runways didn't offer a more dramatic jean but a quieter one: the relaxed, full-length straight leg, worn with a blazer. Paris cut 77% of denim pieces relaxed, the straight leg outgrew the wide leg three to one, and there was a single skinny jean in the whole city. Shoppers were already there: "straight jeans" is searched about three times more than "barrel jeans" and growing faster (+130% to +180% vs +120%), while the barrel is maturing rather than fading (under half its April peak, but above last year every week). Retailers (Gap, Levi's, American Eagle) are selling a family of fits, not a shape.
